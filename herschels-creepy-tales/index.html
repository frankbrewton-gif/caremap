FOR YOUR READING ADVENTURE

The grown-up corner

Read your physical book aloud while Herschel supplies the sounds. Pick a book above, agree on a few listening clues, and let him choose when to tap. There’s no timer, score, or wrong answer.

1. Get comfy

Turn the laptop sideways into landscape. Start at a low Windows volume. Tap a sound to test it.

2. Make some mischief

One sound plays at a time. A new tap restarts or replaces it. Stop sounds or the space bar brings quiet instantly.

3. Go big

Tap Full screen, or press F11 in Edge or Chrome. Escape leaves full screen. Number keys 1–8 also play the pads.

These are real recorded everyday sounds used as story foley: footsteps, crunches, creaks, rattles, and more. The “Scratch, scratch” pad is a gravel scrape, not a recording of a crayon. No ghost voice, thunder, or literal crayon scribble is bundled. You can add any of those below.

Your own recordings

Replace a pad on the current book with a recording from your computer. MP3, WAV, or OGG, up to 10 MB. Use recordings you have permission to use. Imports stay in this browser’s storage when available; keep a copy of your originals.
