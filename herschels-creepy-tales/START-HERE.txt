HERSCHEL’S CREEPY TALES
======================

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted herschels-creepy-tales folder.
3. Double-click index.html. Open with Microsoft Edge or Chrome if asked.
4. Pick a book. Tap a sound! Full screen or F11 makes it bigger.

No installation, account, internet, Python, or Node.js is needed.
Keep the assets folder and the other files together.

Start with your Windows speaker volume LOW.
Tap STOP SOUNDS for instant quiet.

Grown-ups opens instructions and lets you add your own recordings.
README.md has the full guide; SOUND-CREDITS.md has audio licenses.

12 real recorded foley effects are included across 9 boards (72 pads total).

Choose a book from the Tonight’s book menu.
For moving pictures, open Moving pictures & reading.
For narration, Grown-ups > Add a book reading lets you add your own
recording or an audio file you have permission to use. No full-book
narration is included. You can use Windows Sound Recorder to make one.
Ghost voices, thunder, and literal crayon scribbles are not included.
You can import your own recordings into any pad.

Tested with automated Chromium playback tests on Linux. Direct file launch
is the intended Windows method, but Windows hardware was not available
for verification. See TEST-RESULTS.md for exact testing and limits.
